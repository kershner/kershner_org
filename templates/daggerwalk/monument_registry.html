Monument Registry

Permanent landmarks raised by Daggerwalk viewers and eligible as destinations for future quests.

How Monuments Work

How do I earn and place one?

Monuments become available at Pathfinder Renown (200 XP), when you earn your first Monument Token and unlock the first set of types. More tokens and types unlock at major Renown milestones, followed by another token every 30,000 XP after Living Legend. In Twitch chat, use !monument to check your balance, !monument types to draw a short list, and !monument types more to see the remaining unlocked choices.

How do I place and confirm my choice?

Use !monument <type> while the Walker is at the desired site. The bot snapshots that location and gives you 60 seconds to use !monument confirm or !monument cancel. One token is spent only after placement succeeds. The confirmation includes the monument’s description and a direct map link.

Why might placement fail?

You need an available token and enough Renown for the selected type. The captured location must be current, within the Iliac Bay, in wilderness or a town—not the Ocean—and far enough from every existing monument.

What does a monument preserve?

The permanent map location is named for your Twitch account and records your Renown, guild standing, in-game date, season, weather, and time of day when it was raised. It cannot be renamed or moved.

Can monuments become quest destinations?

Yes. Every monument is a new point of interest eligible for future quests. A completed visit is recorded here and in its owner’s Chronicle.
