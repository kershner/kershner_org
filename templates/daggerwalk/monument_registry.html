Monument Registry

Permanent landmarks raised by Daggerwalk viewers and eligible as destinations for future quests.

How do I earn and place a monument?

Your first token and monument types unlock at Pathfinder (200 XP). Major Renown milestones award more; after Living Legend, you earn another token every 30,000 XP. Use !monument to check your balance, !monument types or !monument types more to see unlocked types, and !monument <type> to choose one. Confirm within 60 seconds with !monument confirm; a token is spent only when placement succeeds.

Where can I place one?

In wilderness or a town within the Iliac Bay, outside the Ocean and away from existing monuments. You also need an available token and the Renown required for that type.

What does a monument record?

It permanently records its location, owner, Renown, guild standing, in-game date, season, weather, and time of day. It cannot be renamed or moved, and may become a future quest destination. Completed visits appear here and in the owner’s Chronicle.
