Monument Registry

Permanent landmarks raised by Daggerwalk viewers and eligible as destinations for future quests.

How Monuments Work

How do I earn and place one?

Major Renown milestones award Monument Tokens. In Twitch chat, use !monument types, then !monument place <type> at a valid wilderness or town location and !monument confirm.

What happens when I confirm?

One token is spent only after placement succeeds. The monument becomes a permanent map location named for your Twitch account, recording your Renown, guild standing, date, and conditions at its raising; it cannot be renamed or moved.

Why might placement fail?

The location must be valid, the monument type must be unlocked by your Renown, and the site must be far enough from existing monuments.

Can monuments become quest destinations?

Yes. Every monument is a new point of interest eligible for future quests. A completed visit is recorded here and in its owner’s Chronicle.
